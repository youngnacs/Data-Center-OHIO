# Blueprint — HVAC and liquid cooling

## Constitution

No evaporative **primary**. Closed-loop DLC on every CM. Dry coolers first; adiabatic assist only on extreme Ohio days; metered for WUE.

## Per CM

- CDU N+1, isolate-to-row (autonomous, Q03)
- Pipe and slab for 200 kW/rack
- Local BMS as SoR for liquid (Q05)
- Prefabricated skids (Ohio winter)

## Campus Core

- Makeup, chemistry, glycol storage, fire water **separate** from cooling
- Public WUE extract (Q05)

## Ohio

Ice on dry coolers is a design load. Freeze protection is an IST item. Heat reuse is optional P2+, never a single-point of rejection.

## AI ops

Energy-efficiency agents propose fan/pump bands (supervised). They do not override leak or fire interlocks.
